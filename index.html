How verification works

Each listing and opportunity is checked before it appears: contract source must match on Sourcify (exact_match), the entry transaction must move zero value, and no token approval may be required. Roost Checked means those mechanical checks passed on the date shown — it is not a guarantee of safety or profit. Always re-verify before transacting.
